# Bias and fairness

## Scope
Identifying and managing bias in AI, including the NIST “fair with harmful bias managed” characteristic.

## Documents

- [SP 1270](knowledge/docs/nist-sp-1270.md) — primary bias guidance · [original](input/NIST.SP.1270.pdf)
- [AI RMF 1.0](knowledge/docs/nist-ai-100-1.md) — trustworthy-AI characteristic · [original](input/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf)

## Relationships
SP 1270 is the dedicated bias publication. AI RMF 1.0 folds harmful-bias management into trustworthiness.

## Disagreements and staleness
SP 1270 (2022) predates AI RMF 1.0 (2023). Use both; do not treat SP 1270 as superseded unless a source says so.
