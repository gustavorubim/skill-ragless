# NIST AI RMF Core

## Scope
The four AI RMF functions, trustworthy-AI characteristics, and whether the framework is voluntary. Excludes Northwind-only inventory facts except where they map owners onto the functions.

## Documents

- [AI RMF 1.0](knowledge/docs/nist-ai-100-1.md) — primary definition of GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE · [original](input/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf)
- [Generative AI Profile](knowledge/docs/nist-ai-600-1.md) — GAI actions under the same functions · [original](input/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf)
- [Q3 Model Inventory Briefing](knowledge/docs/q3-model-inventory.md) — Northwind function owners · [original](input/q3-model-inventory.pptx)
- [Northwind AI Governance Policy](knowledge/docs/northwind-ai-governance-policy.md) — internal mapping, including a MEASURE exemption · [original](input/northwind-ai-governance-policy.docx)

## Relationships
Prefer NIST AI RMF 1.0 for what the framework requires or suggests. Prefer Northwind policy for internal controls. The briefing assigns owners; it does not change NIST.

## Disagreements and staleness
Northwind exempts systems under USD 250,000 annual spend from MEASURE. NIST AI RMF 1.0 does not state that exemption.
