# Towards a Standard for Identifying and Managing Bias in Artificial Intelligence

## Purpose
NIST SP 1270 guidance on identifying and managing bias in AI systems.

## Authority and recency
Official NIST special publication. Pre-dates AI RMF 1.0; still the bias-focused companion in this corpus.

## Major topics
- Statistical, computational, and human-system sources of bias
- Harmful bias management as a trustworthiness characteristic
- Voluntary use

## Key definitions, rules, or findings
- Addresses identifying and managing bias rather than declaring a single fairness metric.
- Guidance remains voluntary.

## Notable entities and dates
- NIST; authors include Schwartz, Vassilev, Greene, Perine, Burt, Hall
