# Northwind Model Risk FAQ

## Purpose
Operational FAQ from the Model Risk Management Office (updated 2026-06-15).

## Authority and recency
Internal guidance. Aligns with the governance policy on exception duration.

## Major topics
- Challenger model validation cadence
- Emergency exception expiry
- Contact path

## Key definitions, rules, or findings
- Challenger models follow the same annual independent validation cadence as production models unless MRC approves an emergency exception.
- Emergency exceptions expire after ninety days; a model may not remain in production on an expired exception.
- Contact: model-risk@northwind.example

## Notable entities and dates
- MRC; 2026-06-15
