# Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)

## Purpose
Voluntary NIST framework (January 2023) for organizations that design, develop, deploy, or use AI systems.

## Authority and recency
U.S. government primary framework. Companion GenAI profile is [NIST AI 600-1](knowledge/docs/nist-ai-600-1.md). Review expected no later than 2028.

## Major topics
- Framing AI risk, impacts, and harms
- Characteristics of trustworthy AI
- AI RMF Core: GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE
- Profiles

## Key definitions, rules, or findings
- Intended to be voluntary, rights-preserving, non-sector-specific, and use-case agnostic.
- Trustworthy AI: valid and reliable, safe, secure and resilient, accountable and transparent, explainable and interpretable, privacy-enhanced, fair with harmful bias managed.
- Core functions: GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE.
