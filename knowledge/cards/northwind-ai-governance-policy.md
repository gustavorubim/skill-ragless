# Northwind AI Governance Policy

## Purpose
Internal Northwind Bank control standard for AI/ML systems (NW-AIGP-3.2, effective 2026-04-01).

## Authority and recency
Internal formal policy. Where quieter than law or a formal regulator framework, the stricter requirement wins.

## Major topics
- Independent validation
- Emergency exceptions
- MEASURE function exemption
- Generative AI human review

## Key definitions, rules, or findings
- Production models: independent validation at least annually; MRC is approval authority.
- Emergency exceptions expire after ninety days; no silent renewal.
- AI systems with forecast annual spend under USD 250,000 are exempt from MEASURE (Northwind-only; not in NIST AI RMF 1.0).
- Customer-facing GenAI requires human review before send.

## Notable entities and dates
- Model Risk Committee (MRC); GenAI-Contract-Summarizer (NW-044); 2026-04-01

## Related documents
- [FAQ](knowledge/docs/faq-model-risk.md) — same 90-day exception rule
- [AI RMF 1.0](knowledge/docs/nist-ai-100-1.md) — conflict on MEASURE exemption

## Conflicts or caveats
The MEASURE exemption is explicitly a Northwind cost-control choice and is not stated in NIST AI RMF 1.0.
